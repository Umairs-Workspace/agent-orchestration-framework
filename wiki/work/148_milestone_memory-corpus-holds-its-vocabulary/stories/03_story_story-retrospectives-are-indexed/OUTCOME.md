# 03 · Story retrospectives are indexed — Outcome

## Delivered

### Story lessons are recallable
Every item's `RETROSPECTIVE.md` is indexed, whatever the item's type. A story's lessons carry its ref as `item` (`134/01`), so they are cited as `(m134/01)`, and `--item 134` reaches them. On the live corpus the lesson count rises from 489 to 788, and every one of the 299 new lessons comes from a story folder.
