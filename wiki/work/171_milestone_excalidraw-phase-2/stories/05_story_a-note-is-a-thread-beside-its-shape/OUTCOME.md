# 05 · A note is a thread beside its shape — Outcome

## Delivered

### A note is a thread
A request record carries a `thread` of turns: `reply` appends the operator's turn and reopens the note (a proposed one too, its proposal untouched), `respond` appends the architect's and still sets `answer`, and an older record reads as the thread its request and answer imply; the architect's context carries the whole thread, oldest first.

### Threads beside their shapes
A note on a shape is a pin at that shape's corner — one pin per shape, counting its notes, coloured by whose turn is last — and choosing it opens the thread beside the shape, where a reply is recorded and delivered to the running session through story 04's one `deliver`.

## Assumptions

- **The proposals listing reaches the note** — the listing caps decided notes at 50, so a very old answered note loses its pin (recorded at review).
