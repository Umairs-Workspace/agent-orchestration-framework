# 05 · Notes without a session — Outcome

## Delivered

### Notes that start nothing
Add note records a review request on the selection, or on the whole diagram, without launching a session. The review panel lists every open note, however old, and the operator can withdraw one.

## Gaps

### A contextual way to add a note
- **Status:** open
- **Discharge condition:** a selected shape offers "Add note" on the canvas itself (context menu or selection toolbar).
A note is added only from the bar below the canvas (VERIFICATION F-170-11, deferred by the operator).

### The panel updating when the architect finishes
- **Status:** open
- **Discharge condition:** review records reach the editor over a push channel (phase 2), and the canvas reflects an answer or proposal without a reload.
The editor polls review records every five seconds and the operator refreshed after a review (F-170-13).
