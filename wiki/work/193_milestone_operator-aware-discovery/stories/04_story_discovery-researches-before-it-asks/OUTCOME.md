# 193/04 · Discovery researches before it asks — Outcome

## Delivered

### Operator-aware discovery is a practice of its own
The specification-by-example package declares a second practice, `operator-aware`, on only when `work.examples.enabled` and `work.examples.operatorAware` are both `true`. With it on, its fragments replace every discovery fragment that says `business`: refine's beat, the formulation, the cascade, the hand-back, the Codex entry rule and the PO and architect briefs. The researcher brief gains a fragment of its own, for Claude and for Codex.

### Refine researches a fact before it asks
With the practice on, refine's discovery beat labels each question `domain`, `policy` or `technical`, reads the operator's profile and the item's `domains:`, and sends every `domain` question below `expert` to one researcher pass. That pass writes sourced `## F<n> ·` findings into the research document, and the map cites them as `researched F<n>`.

### What research settles comes back as one batch
The researched examples reach the operator as one batch to skim and object to, not as individual questions. The batch carries no map token.

### Every question explains itself
Every operator question carries `I would pick:`, and below `expert` a `Background:` line that says what the term means, why it matters to the build and what is normal, with a source. A driven ask counts `Background:` toward its 1,500 characters.

### The templates teach the new forms
With the practice on, the installed `EXAMPLES.md` and `RESEARCH.md` templates are its variants. They teach `domain`, `policy` and `researched F<n>`, and the finding's heading and source line.

### Off renders none of it
With the switch absent or `false`, every rendered refine command, PO, researcher and architect brief (Claude and Codex) and both templates are byte-for-byte what a bundle without the practice renders, and hold no sentence only an operator-aware fragment holds (FF-19204). This checkout, with the switch off, renders exactly what it did.
