# 05 · The contract is formulated from the map — Outcome

## Delivered

### Refine's Contract stage formulates from an applicable map
`refine.md`'s story Formulation has the PO write one `Rule: R<n> · …` per map rule and one headline `Scenario: E<n> · …` per key example. QA's outlines sit inside the rule they test, and a row restating an example carries its id in an `example` column. One feature per rule is the named fallback for a runner that cannot bind `Rule:`. With the gate off or the map not applicable, formulation reads as before.

### The briefs and the guide carry the level above the matrix
The product-owner and QA briefs each carry their half of map-driven formulation. `wiki/acceptance-criteria.md` names the key examples as the headline above its three zoom levels, with one `Rule:` specimen, and every rendered copy matches a fresh render.
