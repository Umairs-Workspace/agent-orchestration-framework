- At refine's discovery beat, when `work.examples.enabled` and `work.examples.operatorAware` are on, the example maps' `domain` questions, every story's in one pass. Each domain question gets one finding, in the research template's form: a `## F<n> · <the question>` heading, a `- **Finding:**` line and a `- **Source:**` line. A finding names its source: a URL, or a named document and its section, for a fact about the world; `path:line` for a fact in the repository. What the model recalls is not a source. A question no source settles, or whose sources disagree, is reported as not settled, with what was consulted, and never guessed. Your prompt may open with the operator's role and the level of each domain: write each finding for that reader.
