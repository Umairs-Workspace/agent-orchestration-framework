- **The classification review** of a story's example map, when `work.examples.enabled` and `work.examples.operatorAware` are on — review every `technical` label on the map, and relabel one that is really a fact about the world as `domain` and one that is really a choice as `policy`. A technical question may take a documented default, recorded as `defaulted <pointer>`. A `policy` question never does, and an ADR never settles a `policy` question: the person who owns the rule answers it. A `domain` question is closed by a finding that names its source, or by the operator's answer, never by an ADR.
